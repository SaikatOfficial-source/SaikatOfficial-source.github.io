About

Careful assessment, ethical formulation and evidence-based care, across hospital, university, school and online settings.

My work covers the full clinical workflow: intake and case history, Mental Status Examination, psychometric and projective testing, DSM-5-guided formulation under supervision, and disability and medico-legal documentation.

My research asks why people across rural, sub-urban and urban Bengal explain illness the way they do, and what that means for outreach. I want to build low-cost, culturally grounded services for underserved communities, starting with awareness campaigns in the Bankura region and growing toward a community mental health facility in my hometown.

Approach

Listen first

Every formulation begins with a thorough intake and case history, so a person is understood before they are assessed.

Assess with care

Structured interviews, the Mental Status Examination and validated psychometric and projective tools, always under supervision.

Reach further

Psychoeducation and outreach designed for communities where stigma and uncertainty hold back help-seeking.

Research

The Uncertainty Trap: why distorted minds turn to the supernatural, 2026

A large-scale field study across rural, sub-urban and urban Bengal on why people attribute unexplained illness or behaviour to spirits or curses rather than medical causes.

Finding
The belief is driven by an inability to tolerate not knowing, not by distorted thinking.
Impact
Points to a new, low-cost approach to mental health outreach in underserved Indian communities.
Forgiveness, happiness and emotional well-being, 2025

Explored how forgiveness relates to psychological well-being among college and university students, using standardised measures to identify factors linked to positive mental health.

Women’s safety and healthcare insecurity, 2025

Investigated insecurity around women’s safety and the healthcare system, with reference to the August 2024 RG Kar Hospital incident in Kolkata.
